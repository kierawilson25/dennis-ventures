Design system A/B — which one becomes canonical?

Same components, same markup, rendered under each token set. Decision D1 in PRD.md.

The decision is narrower than the token diff suggests. Of the 13 tokens that differ, most are never referenced by the markup. primary-container — the lavender doing nearly all the visible container work — is #e6e6fa in both. So is secondary (#00a8cc), which drives every CTA, link, and accent rule. What you are actually choosing between is typeface and corner radius, plus a background shift so subtle you will struggle to see it. The scary-looking grey primary and cyan secondary-container in System B appear in the markup once and never, respectively.
